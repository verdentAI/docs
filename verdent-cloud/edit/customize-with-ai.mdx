---
title: Customize with AI Editing
description: Use multi-turn chat, editor, preview, and terminal surfaces to make the generated app yours.
---

After a project is created, Verdent Cloud opens the AI editing workspace. Use natural language to change layout, content, style, behavior, data, and app structure over multiple turns.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a change">
    Tell Verdent what to change, for example “make the hero more playful” or “add a pricing section”.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review edits">
    Inspect changed files, diffs, generated artifacts, and preview output in the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine again">
    Continue with follow-up prompts until the app matches your intent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto fix after publish checks

If publishing fails because of build or security issues and the errors are fixable, Verdent can turn the publish errors into an AI Fix prompt. The agent fixes the issues, and you can publish again.
