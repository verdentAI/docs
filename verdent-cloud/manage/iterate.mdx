---
title: Iterate After Launch
description: Keep improving a published app with AI edits, fixes, and republishing.
---

Publishing is not the end of the workflow. Verdent Cloud is designed for iteration: inspect the live app, review analytics and logs, return to the AI editing workspace, use Auto fix when publish checks fail, and publish again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review what happened">
    Use Analytics and Logs to understand traffic, devices, referrers, and publish issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to editing">
    Continue the same project conversation or start a follow-up task in the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply AI changes">
    Ask Verdent for multi-turn edits, or use Auto fix when publish/build/security errors are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish again">
    Republish or update deployment settings when the app is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>
