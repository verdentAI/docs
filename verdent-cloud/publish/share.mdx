---
title: Share Your Published App
description: Copy your published app link or share it to social platforms.
---

After a successful publish, Verdent Cloud shows your app link and sharing options.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Use the copy button next to the published URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share publicly">
    For Public apps, use the built-in sharing buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Click the live URL to verify what visitors will see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Supported share targets in the current code include Facebook, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, and Reddit.

<Tip>
  Share your app link often, then check [Analytics](/verdent-cloud/insights/analytics) to see how many visitors arrive from the links you share.
</Tip>
